What a class is like

Think Ninja class, but for singing.

Kids learn real pop choruses by echoing, clapping and moving. They're too busy having fun to notice how much they're learning.

Our story

We tried it. We learned. We made it better.

Last fall, we ran our first season with 8 kids, and we watched closely to see what worked.

What workedGames where the kids had real power. When a child's choice changed what happened next, the whole room lit up.
What didn'tAnything where the kids sat still, or only followed along.

How we teach now

  • Repeat after me. I sing, the kids sing it back. No sheet music, no waiting.
  • Rhythm in the body. Kids clap, stomp and move to exciting rhythms.
  • Everyone gets a turn to lead. Throughout each class, kids take turns in small roles: picking who goes first, giving the starting pitch for a song, or pressing the key on the piano.
  • A smaller room. Our first space was big, and focus was hard. This season, we're in a smaller, cozier room, so every kid stays close and engaged.

Meet the teachers

Professional singers. Parents too.

Daniel Berryman

Director

Daniel Berryman is a professional singer with 20 years of experience in many styles. He has performed musical theatre across the country and in NYC. With Greenburg Artists, he has sung The Beatles and Paul Simon with symphonies nationwide. He won the 2019 Lotte Lenya Vocal Competition and now sings with a local opera company in NYC.

Why he started the choir

The idea for this choir began with singing to his own children as babies. For Daniel, singing is a basic human activity that brings connection, joy and healing. Through this choir, he hopes to bring singing together back to children and families in his community and beyond.

Grace Morgan Berryman

Assistant Director

Grace Morgan Berryman is a musical theatre artist and educator, best known for her years touring the country as Christine in The Phantom of the Opera. She brings Broadway experience, heartfelt artistry and a nurturing spirit to every room she enters.

More about Grace

Grace is an acting and voice coach with Broadway Vocal Coach Aspire, where she helps students, especially those preparing for college musical theatre programs, take their next big step. She has also worked extensively in childcare on the Upper West Side and across NYC. As a mother of two young boys, she brings both professional insight and personal care to her work with children and families.
